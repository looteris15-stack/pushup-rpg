Pushup Counter Test

Step 1 of your pushup RPG. Do some pushups and check that only strict reps are counted.

Set up the phone

  • Phone on the floor, 1.5–2.5 m to your side, turned sideways.
  • Your whole body, head to feet, in the picture.
  • Good light. A plain background helps.

A rep counts when

  • Arms are straight at the top
  • Elbows bend to 60° or less at the bottom
  • Body stays in a straight line
  • Knees stay off the floor

You hear a click when you are low enough, a hit for each rep, and a buzz with the reason when a rep does not count.
